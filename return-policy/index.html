Return & Refund Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Yamli Mobile is a free digital service. Contributions are voluntary and help support the project's hosting, maintenance, and development. They are not purchases of physical goods, and there is nothing to return.

Refund requests

Contributions are generally non-refundable. If you made a duplicate payment, entered an incorrect amount, or believe a payment was unauthorized, email support@yamlimobile.com as soon as possible. We will review the details and, when appropriate, issue an approved refund to the original payment method.

Please include the payment date, amount, and the email address used at checkout. Do not send full card numbers or security codes by email.

Recurring contributions

If recurring contributions are offered at checkout, the amount and billing interval will be shown before payment. You may cancel future payments using the cancellation link in your payment confirmation or by contacting support@yamlimobile.com. Cancellation stops future charges; it does not automatically refund earlier contributions.

Payment processing

Payments are processed by the payment provider shown at checkout. Provider processing times may affect when an approved refund appears on your statement.

Contact

Questions about a contribution or this policy? Contact support@yamlimobile.com.
