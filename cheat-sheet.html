tips_and_updates Tips for Franco Typing

  • Numbers 1-9 and - are the most important Franco keys for common Arabic letters
  • Hold Shift for capital letters (A-Z)
  • Double letters create special characters (dd = د, ss = ص)
  • Vowels can be added after the main character (a = ا, i = ي, u = و)
  • Practice typing common words to build muscle memory
